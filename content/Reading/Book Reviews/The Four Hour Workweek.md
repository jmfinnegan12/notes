# The Four Hour Workweek by Tim Ferriss

# Summary
This book had a big impact on me immediately after reading and inspired several lifestyle/routine adjustments (namely [[Information Diet]], [[Dreamlining]], and [[Outsourcing]]), which so far have had a positive impact. After coming off of a workaholic phase (spring/early summer 2025) I read it at the right time in my life.

Inspired by Ferriss's workaholic approach as an entrepreneur, subsequent sabbatical and streamlining/automation of work processes. Format of the book took shape in his lectures to Princeton, his alma mater. 

Lots of practical advice, outside the box thinking, and inspiration. Tone is relatable with some wit and good anecdotes sprinkled in. My one criticism is that Ferriss completely diminishes the value work and operates under the assumption that work is not fulfilling or meaningful and therefore we need to minimize it. Everyone can strive to do their work more effectively, and his ideas are valid and helpful for this goal, but he should acknowledge that work itself can be meaningful and fulfilling. 

Overall highly recommend especially to anyone who is dissatisfied with their job/career, who has other interests/ambitions/aspirations outside of their 9-5, or who is generally willing to defy some conventions and go against the grain.

# Takeaways
- "[[Dreamlining|Dreamline]]" and use the results to define a relative income target, work to achieve that target
	- based on target lifestyle, not arbitrary comparison with the Joneses
	- once achieved, work to maintain the target and get more efficient
- conquering fear = defining fear
	- do one thing every day that you fear
	- Describing fears in writing reduces their impact
- read Seneca
- do what *excites* you - extremely cliche but worth remembering
- [[Prioritizing]]
	- focus on a few, important things that will make a difference, forget the rest and do not let it interfere 
	- Define the single most important task of the day and accomplish it
- Most information is time-consuming, negative, irrelevant to your goals, and outside of your influence.
- get a virtual assistant
	- Take baby steps towards paying others to do work for you
- If you can't define it or act upon it, forget it.
- designate a single space for work and solely work—or you will never be able to escape it - [[Separation of Work and Play]]
- enjoy long meals with large groups of friends
- [[Decision Making]]
	- make decisions quickly
	- Regret is past-tense decision making. Eliminate complaining to minimize regret.
- Do not expect work to fill a void that non-work relationships and activities should.
- meet deadlines above all else
