# For Whom the Bell Tolls by Ernest Hemingway

# Summary
Spanish civil war. Robert Jordan is an American special forces operative tasked with blowing up a bridge, timed with the start of a larger attack by the revolutionary force against the fascists. The book follows his three days in a cave hideout in the mountains with a group of guerrilla fighters who he has teamed up with for the bridge operation. He falls in love with Maria, whose family was killed, her head shaved, and raped/tortured by the fascists, and who has been taken in by the guerrillas. Guerrillas led by Pablo, washed up revolutionary who is highly intelligent but now a cowardly alcoholic. His wife Pilar who is the de facto leader of their operation. Jordan learns of the start of the war when Pablo led an assault on a fort and rallied the townspeople to kill the fascists in the street 
# Themes
- war and the toll it takes on individuals
- lack of empathy, sometimes disdain, from higher level decision makers toward lower level foot soldiers/civilians 
- love -- antiquated story of falling in love, and the rolls Robert Jordan and Maria play in their contrived future relationship
