# The Alchemist by Paulo Coelho

# Summary
Proverb about a Spanish shepherd boy's travels thru Africa across the desert, quest to find treasure aka his purpose in life. Heavy religious influence and pompous tone; takes itself way too seriously. Lots of overused tropes and elements of biblical fantasy (following a recurring dream, controlling the wind, speaking to the sun, alchemist actually creates gold, etc.) that conflict with the modern and otherwise realistic setting (late 1800s?) 

"when you really want something to happen, the whole universe will conspire so that your wish comes true" is probably the core message and imo stupid, condescending advice that is not practical or even true in many cases

Short read but waste of time, do not recommend
