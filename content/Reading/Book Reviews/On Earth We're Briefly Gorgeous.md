# On Earth We're Briefly Gorgeous by Ocean Vuong

# Summary
Long form / lyrical letter from Vietnamese-American son to his mother. Graphic, raw views of the horrors of the Vietnam war and how the resulting trauma manifests itself in the participants (on both sides) later in life and in their families and descendants, poor Asian immigrants trying to make it and raise a family in America, harsh reality of drugs and poverty in US cities, and the coming of age story identity confusion and moments that shattered the innocence of a gay young man with this background. 

Avant-garde style, dark tone, intense imagery and motifs. Purports to not conform to a single "genre" of fiction or nonfiction. 

I had mixed feelings. At times it was deeply moving. Level of graphic detail pushed me out of my comfort zone. Other times it seemed like Vuong tried to be overly nonconformist in his style and lost me. Overall recommend. 
# Themes
- horrors of war and ensuing generational family trauma
- single parent households
- immigrant struggles
- poverty
- drugs
- coming of age
- gay identity
