# All the Light We Cannot See by Anthony Doerr

# Summary
Tells parallel stories, nonlinear chronology, of two children confronted with the horrors of WWII. Touching, gripping story. Writing style concisely conveys deep meaning and emotion thru imagery and dialogue. Best book I have read in 2025 so far. 

Marie-Laure is a blind girl in Paris forced to evacuate with her dutiful father after the Nazis invade France. Goes to Saint-Malo on the coast of France to stay with her great uncle, gets involved in the French resistance. Werner is an orphan in a coal mining town, extremely bright, loves radios, gets accepted to a prestigious Nazi military academy and becomes an officer as a teenager in charge of triangulating and eliminating enemy guerrilla radio units. 

Took Doerr 10 years to write! Inspired by overhearing a frustrated passenger after his phone call was disconnected in 2004 on a train ride; Doerr felt he did not sufficiently appreciate the "miracle" of technology. Wanted to write a novel in appreciation of the miracle. Decided to set in WWII, focus on Battle of Saint-Malo. Highly regarded including Pulitzer Prize for fiction.
