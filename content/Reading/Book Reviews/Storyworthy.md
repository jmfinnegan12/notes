# Storyworthy by Matthew Dicks

# Summary
Provides techniques for finding, crafting, and telling stories that connect with audiences. Written by a teacher, novelist, blogger, competitive storytelling champion who now teaches storytelling seminars and classes. Has lived an extremely interesting and challenging life but emphasizes that the best stories are relatable to audiences and often about small moments, not about grand accomplishments. He provides compelling reasons for why storytelling is a valuable skill (business, social, family, etc) and advice for finding and telling stories centered on self reflection and self awareness. Overall good read, interesting examples in the book, motivated me to organize and document my own stories and begin practicing this skill that I know I am not good at.
