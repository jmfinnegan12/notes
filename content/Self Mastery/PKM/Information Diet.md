# Information Diet
#mindfulness #productivity 

The ability to *think* is of much greater value than the ability to memorize facts
> "Learning to ignore things is one of the greatest paths to inner peace" -Robert J. Sawyer

>"Why should I fill my brain with facts I can find in two minutes in any standard reference book?" -Albert Einstein

## Tracking
Metacognition 
- Develop the habit of asking yourself, “Will I definitely use this information for something immediate and important?”
- ask why am I consuming this? is it relevant? is it high quality? does it cause anxiety, stress, overwhelm? is it worth my time? 
- practice the art of "non-finishing"
	- Starting something doesn't automatically justify finishing it
Keep an Information Journal
- track time, reduce/eliminate unimportant info sources, iterate
