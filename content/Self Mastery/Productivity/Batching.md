# Batching
Scheduling repetitive and necessary time consuming tasks
- at the lowest possible frequency
- back to back with each other 
In order to 
- minimize the amount of time that they consume
- still accomplish them effectively

## Examples
general "admin" types of tasks for personal and business
- mail - check it weekly instead of daily, batch snail mail and email
- laundry - wait for full load
- groceries - meal prep
- payments - pay all bills at once
