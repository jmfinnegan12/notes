# Outsourcing

[[Virtual Assistant]]

Give people responsibility
Indicate that you trust them
Empower others to act without interrupting you
- Even if you CAN do something better it doesn't mean that you SHOULD be doing it

You can always do something more cheaply yourself. This doesn't mean that you want to spend your time doing it
- Eg if spend your time worth $20/hr on a task that you can outsource to someone else for $10/hr, it is a poor use of resources

## Eliminate before you delegate
Unless something is well defined and important, no one should do it

## Rules of Delegating
1. Each delegated task must be both time-consuming and well-defined. 
	1. If you’re running around like a chicken with its head cut off and assign your VA to do that for you, it doesn’t improve the order of the universe.
2. Have fun with it
	1. Have someone in Bangalore or Shanghai send e-mails to friends as your personal concierge to set lunch dates or similar basics. 
	2. Harass your boss with odd phone calls in strong accents from unknown numbers. 
	3. Being effective doesn’t mean being serious all the time. It’s fun being in control for a change. Get a bit of repression off your chest so it doesn’t turn into a complex later.
