# Avoiding Unnecessary Complexity

Why do we make things more complex than they need to be?
- desire to control inherently unpredictable situations
- complexity bias - we perceive more complex systems are higher quality even if they are not
- adding "just in case" features

Why this is dangerous?
- huge waste of time and energy
- leads to burnout, disenchantment, abandoning projects

How to avoid?
- [[Occam's Razor]]
