# Idea Incubator
Inspired by this [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tp-Ez0shm9c) from Mike Schmitz -- great Obsidian and [[Personal Knowledge Management|PKM]] content creator. 

## Concept
It is easy for people to have ideas. It is difficult to implement them. The "seed" of an idea is more likely to grow into a "flower" of a finished product if one cultivates it. 

The idea incubator concept is my stage for cultivating ideas as well as my active project management homepage. It facilitates capturing new ideas, organizing them, working on the ones that become priorities, and periodically revisiting those that are not currently priorities to cultivate them.

# My Idea Incubator Organization Structure
## Active 
Ideas I am currently prioritizing. I try to have no more than five active ideas at once. I split these into the following phases:
1. Idea - raw phase, analogous to an inbox
2. Draft - design phase
3. Prototype - first draft
4. Implementation - initial deployment
5. Mature - beyond initial deployment

## Future
Ideas that are likely to become a priority in the next 4-6 weeks

## Inactive 
Ideas that are not active or likely to become a priority in the next 4-6 weeks. I categorize these:
1. Completed
2. Idle
3. Recycle Bin
